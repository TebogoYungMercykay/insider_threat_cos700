# Code of Conduct

![Title slide of the project presentation](docs/assets/readme.png)

## Our Commitment

This project is committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, race, ethnicity, age, religion or nationality.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in every interaction.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for your words and actions.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination in any form.
- Personal attacks, insults or derogatory comments.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews or discussions.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every space of the project, including the repository, its issues, pull requests and reviews, and any other channel used to discuss it.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to the maintainer privately through GitHub. Every report is reviewed promptly and handled in confidence.

## Enforcement

Violations may lead to a warning, a temporary block from the project's spaces, or a permanent ban, in proportion to the severity of the behaviour.
